Critical AC Emergencies
We Respond To Immediately

If you experience any of these severe symptoms, turn off the AC isolator switch and call our helpline immediately.

🔥

1. Burning Smell / Smoke

Electrical arcing, burnt insulation wire, or short circuit.

Call SOS Now →
⚡

2. Main Breaker Tripping

AC immediately trips MCB when turned on (ground fault).

Call SOS Now →
🌊

3. Severe Water Inundation

Water cascading over expensive wooden flooring or electronics.

Call SOS Now →
☀️

4. AC Dead in Peak Heat

Complete shutdown in 43°C heat with elderly or infants.

Call SOS Now →
💨

5. Refrigerant Gas Cloud

Sudden loud hiss with oily chemical fog from pipes.

Call SOS Now →
⚙️

6. Compressor Seizure

Heavy hum with high current draw and instant thermal cut.

Call SOS Now →
🔊

7. Loud Clanking / Grinding

Violent metal impact inside fan chamber or outdoor unit.

Call SOS Now →
🖥️

8. Server Room Cooling Failure

Critical temperature spike threatening IT infrastructure.

Call SOS Now →
