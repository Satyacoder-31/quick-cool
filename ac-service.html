The Science of Servicing

Why Regular AC Servicing
Is Essential For Your Home

An unserviced AC doesn't just cool poorly — it recirculates allergens and wastes thousands of rupees every month in electricity.

🌬️

Pure, Clean Breathing Air

Our antimicrobial foam wash purges black mold spores, bacteria, pollen, and trapped fine PM2.5 particulates from deep within the cooling fins.

⚡

Up to 30% Lower Power Bills

Unclogged coils allow unrestricted heat exchange. The compressor cuts off up to 15 minutes faster per cycle, dramatically trimming monthly electricity costs.

❄️

Instant Ice-Cold Chilling

Restores the factory-designed airflow CFM and grille discharge temperature to between 14°C and 16°C for near-instant room cooling.

🛡️

Extends Compressor Lifespan

Compressor overheating caused by dirty outdoor condenser fins is the #1 cause of fatal AC burnouts. Deep servicing protects your most expensive part.

💧

Zero Water Dripping On Walls

We flush the internal drain trap and condensate tubing with high-velocity water jets to prevent sludge accumulation that causes room wall stains.

🤫

Whisper-Quiet Operation

Cleaning dust caked onto the cylindrical cross-flow blower fan restores dynamic balance, eliminating subtle vibrations and high-speed whining noises.
