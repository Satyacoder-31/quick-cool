Engineering Quality Control

The QuickCool 9-Point
Installation Checklist

Every installation follows strict technical protocols to ensure safety, maximum cooling, and zero wall vibration.

📐

1. Laser Spirit Leveling

Millimeter-precise alignment of the indoor unit ensures condensate flows naturally into the drain tray and prevents internal room water leakage.

🧱

2. Diamond Core Drilling

Neat, low-dust wall core cutting angled downwards at 10° for proper drainage slope without damaging your home's plaster or paint.

🛠️

3. Zero-Vibration Brackets

Heavy-duty GI powder-coated outdoor wall brackets anchored with high-tensile steel fasteners and thick industrial rubber vibration isolator pads.

🔥

4. Nitrogen Leak Pressure Test

Pressurizing the copper circuit with 350 PSI dry nitrogen before charging gas to guarantee zero micro-leaks at flared brass union nuts.

⏳

5. Two-Stage Vacuum Purging

Degassing lines using a dual-stage rotary vane vacuum pump down to 500 microns, evacuating all atmospheric moisture and non-condensable air.

⚡

6. Dedicated Electrical Isolator

Verification of voltage drop, heavy-duty 3-core pure copper wiring, 16A/25A MCB safety trip compatibility, and grounding earth test.

💧

7. Drain Overflow Test

Pouring 2 liters of water directly into the internal drain pan to verify smooth gravity discharge without airlocks or wall seepage.

🌬️

8. Thermal Grille Calibration

Measuring delta-T across intake and discharge louvers to confirm the refrigerant cycle meets optimal thermodynamics (14°C - 16°C).

🧹

9. Pristine Site Clean-Up

Vacuuming drill dust, wiping handprints from unit panels, sealing wall hole with weatherproof silicone putty, and hauling packaging waste.
