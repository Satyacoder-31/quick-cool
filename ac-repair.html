Root Cause & Resolution Guide

Common AC Problems & How QuickCool Fixes Them

Transparent engineering insights so you know exactly what is happening inside your air conditioning equipment.

Critical Cooling Loss

AC Not Cooling (Warm Air Blowing)

Possible Causes: Clogged condenser coil fins, low refrigerant charge (gas leak), faulty running capacitor, or compressor thermal overload trip.

QuickCool Solution: We measure digital manifold pressures, perform nitrogen leak testing on copper flares, repair micro-punctures, and vacuum refill pure R32/R410A by exact manufacturer weight.

Book This Fix
Interior Water Damage

Water Leaking Inside Room

Possible Causes: Algae/biofilm clogged condensate drain tray, disconnected drain pipe slope, cracked drain pan, or indoor coil freezing.

QuickCool Solution: High-pressure nitrogen drain flush, antimicrobial chemical coil rinse, indoor unit re-leveling with laser spirit level, and drain slope correction.

Book This Fix
Micro-Electronics

Inverter AC PCB Failure & Error Codes

Possible Causes: Voltage spikes, burnt IPM power module, insect intrusion on motherboard, or communication loss between indoor and outdoor boards.

QuickCool Solution: Advanced multimeter oscilloscope tracing, component-level micro-soldering, conformal anti-moisture coating, or OEM replacement with 90-day warranty.

Book This Fix
Acoustic Disturbance

Loud Rattling or Screeching Noise

Possible Causes: Broken blower fan wheel bearing, loose outdoor mounting bolts, worn-out compressor dampener grommets, or debris inside fan blades.

QuickCool Solution: Precision dynamic fan balancing, installation of heavy-duty silicone anti-vibration isolation pads, and high-temperature motor bearing lubrication.
